Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Poker Hand Guide ("the App") handles information when you use the application.

The App is developed and provided by JCraft Studio ("Developer", "we", or "us").

We aim to minimize the collection of personal information while providing useful poker hand reference and comparison features.

Important Notice

Poker Hand Guide is an educational reference and hand comparison utility.

The App does not provide real-money betting, gambling transactions, casino services, cash rewards, wagering features, or guaranteed winning predictions.

No real money or items of monetary value can be wagered through the App.

1. Information We Collect

Poker Hand Guide does not require users to create accounts or sign in.

The App does not directly request personal identity information such as:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Home address
  • Government identification number
  • Payment card or banking information
  • Precise geographic location

The App may store certain non-account-related information locally on your device to support its functionality.

  • Selected language
  • Display or theme preferences
  • Recent poker hand comparison history

These records are not automatically uploaded to a server operated by JCraft Studio.

However, third-party advertising services may independently collect or process certain device and advertising-related information, as described below.

2. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Poker Hand Guide uses third-party advertising technology to support the operation and continued development of the free application.

The App uses Unity LevelPlay as its advertising mediation platform.

Advertising services integrated through the mediation platform may include:

  • Unity LevelPlay - Advertising mediation and monetization management
  • Unity Ads - Advertisement delivery, measurement, and optimization
  • ironSource Ads - Advertisement delivery, measurement, and optimization

Depending on SDK configuration, device settings, user choices, region, and applicable law, these services may process information such as:

  • Android Advertising ID, where available and permitted

  • Device model, manufacturer, operating system version, and device language
  • Application identifiers, SDK identifiers, and app installation information
  • Advertising requests, impressions, clicks, and rewarded-ad interactions
  • App activity associated with advertising services
  • Diagnostic information, technical logs, and crash-related information

These are examples of information that may be processed. Not every category is necessarily collected on every device.

Such information may be used to display advertisements, measure ad performance, prevent advertising fraud, maintain service security, and improve advertising systems.

Advertising may be personalized or non-personalized depending on applicable legal requirements, device settings, supported consent mechanisms, and the user's available choices.

Advertising providers may process or share relevant information with their service providers, advertising partners, and other authorized recipients as described in their respective privacy policies.

JCraft Studio may access aggregated advertising performance statistics through the advertising dashboard.

For additional details, please review the following official privacy policies:

  • Unity Privacy Policy
  • Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy

Unity's privacy documentation also covers relevant Unity Ads, LevelPlay, and affiliated advertising services.

3. Local App Data

Poker Hand Guide may save recent poker hand comparison records, language preferences, display settings, and other app preferences locally on your device.

These records are used to provide app functionality and restore previously selected preferences.

JCraft Studio does not automatically receive your selected poker hands or locally stored comparison history through a Developer-operated server.

You may delete locally stored information by:

Device backup and restore features may affect whether locally stored data can be restored after reinstalling the App.
