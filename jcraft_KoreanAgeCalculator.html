저장 및 즐겨찾기 기능을 사용하는 경우 다음 정보가 기기에 저장될 수 있습니다.

This Privacy Policy explains how Korean Age Calculator ("the App"), provided by JCraft Studio, handles information.

The App calculates international age, Korean year-age, and related information based on the date of birth entered.

Developer contact information is available on the App's Google Play Store listing.

No account sign-in is required.

1.1 User-entered information

  • Names or nicknames
  • Dates of birth (Gregorian calendar)
  • Favorites and sorting preferences
  • Language and App settings

Saved information is stored locally on the user's device and is not transmitted to a developer-operated server.

1.2 Advertising information

The App uses Unity LevelPlay advertising mediation and Unity Ads for banner and interstitial advertising.

Unity and related advertising providers may process:

  • Android Advertising ID or similar identifiers
  • IP address and network information
  • Device model and operating system
  • App identifier and version
  • Ad requests, impressions, clicks and interactions
  • Ad measurement and fraud-prevention information
  • Technical diagnostics and errors

IP addresses may be used to estimate approximate location. Actual processing depends on SDK configuration, device settings, privacy choices, location, and applicable laws.

1.3 Diagnostics

Basic diagnostic features provided by Android or the distribution platform may be used to improve App stability.

2. How Information Is Used

  • Calculate international and Korean year-age
  • Display saved people and favorites
  • Maintain App preferences
  • Display and measure advertisements
  • Prevent advertising fraud
  • Improve reliability and diagnose errors

Zodiac animals are calculated by birth year, not lunar new year cutoffs.

The Developer does not intentionally transmit or sell users' locally stored names, birth dates, or favorites to advertising providers.

However, Unity LevelPlay, Unity Ads, and related advertising partners may process or share device identifiers and advertising-related information.

4. Local Storage and Deletion

Saved names, birth dates, favorites, and settings are stored locally on the device.

Users may delete records using available in-App features. Clearing App data or uninstalling generally removes App-specific data.

Some data may be restored through Android backup and restore features. Advertising-related information is retained under provider policies.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not primarily directed to children and does not require account registration or submission of personal information for its core features.

Advertising-related processing is subject to applicable laws and provider privacy policies.

6. Your Choices

  • Delete saved entries and favorites
  • Clear App data or uninstall the App
  • Manage advertising ID and privacy settings
  • Exercise applicable privacy rights
