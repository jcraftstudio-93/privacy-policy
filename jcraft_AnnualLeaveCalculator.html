1. 개요

본 개인정보처리방침은 JCraft Studio (이하 "개발자")가 제공하는 Annual Leave Calculator (연차계산기) (이하 "앱")에서 개인정보가 어떻게 처리되는지 안내합니다.

본 앱은 연차 계산 및 휴가 기록 관리를 위한 유틸리티 애플리케이션입니다. 별도의 회원가입이나 로그인 없이 사용할 수 있습니다.

연차, 반차, 특별휴가 기록 및 날짜 메모, 언어 설정 등 앱의 주요 데이터는 사용자 기기 내부의 로컬 저장소에 저장됩니다.

앱 자체는 이용자의 휴가 기록 및 메모를 개발자 서버로 전송하지 않습니다. 다만 광고 제공을 위해 사용하는 Unity LevelPlay 및 Unity Ads에서 일부 기기 정보와 광고 관련 데이터를 처리할 수 있습니다.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how JCraft Studio ("Developer") handles information in Annual Leave Calculator ("App").

The App is a utility for calculating annual leave and managing leave records. It can be used without creating an account or signing in.

Leave records, date notes, language preferences, and other app settings are primarily stored locally on the user's device.

The App does not upload your leave records or notes to Developer-operated servers. However, Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads may process certain device information and advertising data.

2. Information We Collect

The App itself does not require registration and does not directly collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or similar personal identifiers.

The following information may be stored locally to provide App features:

  • Company information and granted annual leave days
  • Annual leave, half-day leave, and special leave records
  • Leave dates and date-specific notes
  • Language preferences and app settings

This information is used to provide leave calculations, record management, and user preferences.

Advertising SDKs (Unity LevelPlay / Unity Ads) may automatically collect or process certain information, including:

  • Advertising identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location inferred from it
  • Device manufacturer, model, and operating system information
  • App version and advertising-related app usage information
  • Ad impressions, clicks, and viewing information
  • Technical data used for ad measurement and fraud prevention
  • Error and diagnostic information related to advertising services

The specific information processed may vary depending on SDK configuration, device settings, privacy choices, and applicable laws.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be processed for the following purposes:

  • Calculating annual leave and remaining leave balances
  • Managing annual, half-day, and special leave records
  • Providing calendar and date-specific note features
  • Maintaining language preferences and app settings
  • Displaying banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements
  • Managing ad frequency and measuring ad performance
  • Preventing advertising fraud
  • Maintaining the stability of the App and advertising services

4. Advertising and Third-Party Services

The App uses Unity LevelPlay as an advertising mediation platform and Unity Ads to support its free services.

Advertising formats may include:

  • Banner ads
  • Interstitial ads
  • Rewarded ads

Rewarded advertisements may allow users to voluntarily watch an ad in exchange for temporary ad-free benefits or other in-app rewards.

Unity and its advertising partners may collect or process device and advertising-related information for ad delivery, measurement, optimization, and fraud prevention.

The Developer does not sell or rent locally stored leave records or date notes for advertising purposes.

For more information, please review the following official policies:

  • Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy
  • Unity Privacy FAQ

Information processed by advertising SDKs is subject to the privacy policies of the relevant advertising service providers and applicable laws.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Annual leave, half-day leave, special leave records, and notes are stored locally on your device.
  • You can delete leave records using the App's record deletion functionality.
  • You may remove locally stored data by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App.
  • Some information may remain in operating system backups, depending on device settings.
  • Retention periods and deletion procedures for advertising data are governed by the relevant advertising providers' privacy policies.

6. Permissions and Security

The App may use internet and network connectivity to load advertisements.

We aim to minimize unnecessary data transmission by storing core leave records locally and applying reasonable safeguards.

However, no security method can guarantee complete protection against device loss, malware, or system vulnerabilities.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is a general annual leave management utility and is not designed for children under 13.

The Developer does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13.

Advertising-related data is handled according to applicable laws and the privacy policies of the advertising service providers.

8. Your Choices and Rights

You may manage advertising identifiers and related privacy preferences through your device's privacy settings.

Unity Ads may provide privacy controls within advertisements that allow users to manage certain advertising data processing choices.

You may delete locally stored information through the App's deletion features or your device's app data settings.

Requests to access, delete, or restrict advertising-related personal data may be submitted through the procedures described in Unity's privacy policy.

9. Data Retention Period

Leave records and notes may remain on your device until you delete them or remove the App's data.

The Developer does not store this information in separate user accounts or on Developer-operated servers.

Advertising data retention periods are determined by Unity and the relevant advertising providers in accordance with their privacy policies and applicable laws.
