Privacy Policy

For Medication Reminder

Effective date: November 9, 2026
Last updated: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Medication Reminder ("the App"), provided by JCraft Studio, accesses, uses, stores, backs up, and otherwise processes information when you use the App.

1. Scope of this Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to Medication Reminder and its features, including medication schedules, completion records, prescription records, notifications, home-screen widgets, local backup, Google Drive backup, image attachments, and advertising services.

Where consent is required by applicable law, the App or the relevant third-party service may request consent separately.

2. Information Entered or Generated by Users

Depending on the features used, the App may process the following information:

  • Medication names, dosage information, medication times, start and end dates, repeat schedules, selected weekdays, and personal notes.
  • Medication completion status and medication history.
  • Hospital or clinic name, doctor name, telephone number, visit date, treatment cost, medicine cost, prescription details, and related notes.
  • Prescription images, receipt images, medicine images, or other images selected by the user.
  • Language, theme, notification, vibration, sound, advance reminder, repeat reminder, and automatic backup preferences.
  • Backup metadata, including backup date, application version, and backup format version.

This information is provided directly by users or generated as a result of their use of the App.

Medication and prescription records may contain sensitive health-related personal information. Users should take appropriate care when storing, exporting, or backing up these records.

3. Local Storage

Medication schedules, completion history, prescription records, App settings, and related data are primarily stored locally on the user's device.

Images selected by the user may be copied into the App's private storage directory so that records can remain available if the original image is moved or deleted.

The Developer does not operate a separate server for centrally collecting or storing medication or prescription records.

This does not prevent users from voluntarily storing backup copies in Google Drive through the optional backup functions described below.

4. Notifications and Home-Screen Widgets

If notifications are enabled, the App uses medication schedule information to create reminders at selected times.

Notification content may include medication names, dosage, and scheduled times.

Home-screen widgets may display the next medication, remaining time, completion status, or today's schedule.

Notifications and widgets are generated from locally stored App information.

Their contents may be visible on the device's lock screen or home screen, depending on Android privacy settings.

Medication notifications may also appear on a connected smartwatch when permitted by the phone, watch, and notification settings.

The App does not provide a separate Wear OS application unless explicitly stated in its store listing.

5. Device Permissions

The App may use the following permissions or system capabilities when required by enabled features.

Permission or capability Purpose
Notifications Medication reminders, advance reminders, and repeat reminders.
Exact or scheduled alarms Delivering reminders at or near user-selected times, subject to Android system controls.
Vibration Vibrating the device when enabled by the user.
Photo or media selection Selecting prescription, receipt, or medication-related images.
Internet Loading advertisements and using optional Google Sign-In and Google Drive backup functions.
Device boot events Restoring medication reminders and updating widgets after restart.

Users may deny or revoke permissions through Android settings. Some features may not function correctly without their required permissions.

6. Local JSON Backup and Restore

The App may allow users to export medication records, prescription records, App settings, and related metadata as a local JSON backup file.

Exported files may be accessible to the user, operating system, file-management applications, cloud-storage providers, or applications selected by the user.

Users are responsible for protecting exported backup files containing sensitive medication or prescription information.

7. Google Sign-In and Google Drive Backup

The App may provide optional Google Sign-In and Google Drive backup and restore functions.

These functions are not required to use the App's core local features.

When signing in, the App may receive limited Google account information required for authentication, such as an account identifier, display name, or email address, depending on the permissions granted.

When Google Drive backup is enabled, backup information may be uploaded to the user's selected Google Drive account.

Depending on the implemented options, backups may contain:

  • Medication schedules and completion history
  • Prescription and hospital records
  • App settings
  • Backup metadata
  • Prescription, receipt, or medication images where image backup is supported and selected.

Google Drive access is intended to be limited to files created or selected for use by the App, consistent with the permissions requested.

Manual backups occur when initiated by the user. Automatic backups may occur when explicitly enabled by the user while signed in.

The Developer does not receive or store the user's Google password.

Google authentication and Drive services are operated by Google.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Users may revoke the App's Google account access through their Google Account settings.

Revoking access may disable Drive backup and restore but does not automatically delete data stored locally on the device or backup files already stored in Drive.

8. Advertising - Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads

The App uses Unity LevelPlay advertising mediation and Unity Ads to display advertisements and support the App's free operation.

8.1 Advertising Formats

Depending on the App version and advertising configuration, banner and interstitial advertisements may be displayed.

Advertisements are provided through configured advertising networks managed using Unity LevelPlay.

8.2 Advertising Data Processing

Unity and related advertising providers may process certain information for advertisement delivery, measurement, frequency management, security, and fraud prevention.

Such information may include:

  • Android Advertising ID and other advertising-related identifiers.
  • IP address and approximate location inferred from IP information.
  • Device model, operating system, App identifier, and App version.
  • Advertising requests, impressions, clicks, views, and interactions.
  • Technical diagnostics, performance information, and advertising errors.
  • Consent or privacy-choice information, where applicable.

Actual data processing may vary depending on SDK configuration, connected advertising networks, device settings, location, privacy choices, and applicable laws.

Health information and advertising

The App is not designed to transmit medication names, dosage information, prescription images, medication history, or hospital records to Unity LevelPlay or Unity Ads for advertising targeting.

These health-related records are separate from the device and advertising information that advertising SDKs may process.

8.3 Advertising Privacy Choices

Users may manage available advertising privacy settings through Android device settings and any privacy controls provided by Unity or its advertising partners.

Depending on the Android version, users may reset or delete their Advertising ID.

Restricting personalized advertisements does not necessarily prevent non-personalized ads from appearing.

9. Data Sharing

The Developer does not sell medication records, prescription records, or attached medical images.

Information may be processed or transmitted in the following situations:

  • To Google when the user enables Google Sign-In or Google Drive backup.
  • To Unity LevelPlay, Unity Ads, and connected advertising partners for advertising delivery and measurement.
  • To an application or service selected by the user when exporting, sharing, opening, or uploading a backup or image.
  • Where required by applicable law, valid legal process, or lawful government requests.

The Developer does not intentionally provide user-entered health records to advertising SDKs for targeted advertising.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored information generally remains on the device until the user:

  • Deletes individual medication or prescription records.
  • Clears App data in Android settings.
  • Uninstalls the App.
  • Replaces existing data through backup restoration.

Exported files, Google Drive backups, and files shared with other services may remain after uninstalling the App.

Users must manage or delete these files through the relevant file manager, Google Drive account, or external service.

Advertising data processed by Unity or connected providers is retained and deleted under their respective policies.

The App does not require an independent developer-managed account.

11. Data Security

The Developer uses reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information processed.

However, no device, storage method, internet transmission, or backup service can guarantee complete security.

Users should protect their devices, Google accounts, exported backups, and screen-lock credentials.

12. Children's Privacy

The App is a general medication-management utility and is not specifically directed to children.

Parents or legal guardians should supervise use by children and manage medication information entered into the App.

The Developer does not intentionally request children's personal information for independent marketing or profiling.

13. Medical Disclaimer

Important Medical Notice

The App is provided only as a medication schedule and record-management tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, prescriptions, dosage recommendations, or emergency medical services.

Users must follow the instructions of qualified doctors, pharmacists, or other healthcare professionals.

Users should not start, stop, change, or delay medication based solely on information displayed by the App.

Reminder delivery may be delayed or prevented by Android restrictions, battery optimization, permissions, device settings, smartwatch settings, or technical failures.

The App must not be used as the only means of managing critical or life-sustaining medication.

In a medical emergency, contact local emergency services or a qualified healthcare professional.

본 개인정보처리방침은 JCraft Studio가 제공하는 복약 알림 앱(이하 "앱")에서 사용자의 정보를 어떠한 방식으로 접근·이용·저장·백업 및 처리하는지 설명합니다.

1. 적용 범위

본 개인정보처리방침은 복약 일정, 복용 완료 기록, 처방 기록, 알림, 홈 화면 위젯, 로컬 백업, Google Drive 백업, 이미지 첨부 및 광고를 포함한 앱의 관련 기능에 적용됩니다.

관련 법률상 별도의 동의가 필요한 경우 앱 또는 해당 제3자 서비스가 별도의 동의를 요청할 수 있습니다.

2. 사용자가 입력하거나 생성하는 정보

사용자가 선택한 기능에 따라 앱은 다음 정보를 처리할 수 있습니다.

  • 약 이름, 복용량, 복용시간, 시작일과 종료일, 반복 일정, 반복 요일 및 사용자 메모
  • 복용 완료 상태 및 복용 이력
  • 병원 또는 의료기관명, 의사 이름, 전화번호, 방문일, 진료비, 약제비, 처방 내역 및 관련 메모
  • 처방전 사진, 영수증 사진, 약 사진 및 사용자가 선택한 이미지
  • 언어, 테마, 알림, 진동, 소리, 미리 알림, 재알림, 자동 백업 등의 앱 설정
  • 백업 일시, 앱 버전, 백업 파일 형식 버전 등의 메타데이터

해당 정보는 사용자가 직접 입력하거나 앱 사용 과정에서 생성됩니다.

복약 기록과 처방전 등에는 건강 관련 민감한 개인정보가 포함될 수 있으므로 사용자는 저장 및 백업 시 적절한 보호 조치를 취해야 합니다.

3. 기기 내부 저장

복약 일정, 복용 이력, 처방 기록, 앱 설정 및 관련 데이터는 기본적으로 사용자 기기 내부에 저장됩니다.

사용자가 선택한 이미지는 원본 이미지가 이동되거나 삭제되어도 기록을 유지할 수 있도록 앱 전용 내부 저장공간에 복사될 수 있습니다.

개발자는 복약 기록이나 처방 기록을 수집하여 중앙에서 저장하기 위한 별도의 자체 서버를 운영하지 않습니다.

다만 사용자가 Google Drive 백업을 선택한 경우 관련 데이터가 Google Drive로 전송될 수 있습니다.

4. 알림 및 홈 화면 위젯

사용자가 알림을 활성화하면 앱은 등록된 복약 일정에 따라 지정된 시간에 알림을 생성합니다.

알림에는 약 이름, 복용량 및 예정시간 등이 표시될 수 있습니다.

홈 화면 위젯에는 다음 복약 일정, 남은 시간, 복용 상태 또는 오늘의 복약 일정이 표시될 수 있습니다.

알림 및 위젯 정보는 기본적으로 기기에 저장된 데이터를 바탕으로 생성됩니다.

시스템 설정에 따라 이러한 정보가 잠금 화면이나 홈 화면에서 다른 사람에게 보일 수 있습니다.

추가적인 보호가 필요한 사용자는 Android 알림 및 잠금 화면의 개인정보 보호 설정을 조정해야 합니다.

휴대전화와 스마트워치의 연결 상태 및 알림 설정에 따라 복약 알림이 연결된 스마트워치에도 표시될 수 있습니다.

스토어 등록정보에 별도로 명시하지 않는 한 본 앱은 독립적인 Wear OS 전용 애플리케이션을 제공하지 않습니다.
