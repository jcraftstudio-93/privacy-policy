This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in the Korean Military Discharge Calculator application (the "App").

The App is an independent utility designed to help users calculate and manage estimated military discharge dates, service countdowns, progress, and leave schedules based on South Korean military service periods.

This App is not an official application created, provided, approved, sponsored, or operated by the Government of the Republic of Korea, the Ministry of National Defense, the Military Manpower Administration, any branch of the armed forces, or any other government or public authority.

1. Information Processed by the App

To provide its main features, the App may store information entered or selected by the user locally on the user's device, including:

  • Enlistment date
  • Military service type
  • Leave schedules and related information entered by the user
  • Language preferences
  • Dark mode and other application preferences
  • Local settings required for application functionality
  • Calculated values and settings required for the home screen widget

This information is stored locally on the user's device for application functionality. It is not directly transmitted to or stored on a separate server operated by the developer, nor is it linked to a developer-managed user account.

2. Purposes of Local Data Processing

Information stored locally may be used to:

  • Calculate an estimated military discharge date
  • Calculate remaining military service time
  • Display a discharge D-Day and countdown
  • Calculate military service progress
  • Store and display leave schedules
  • Provide home screen widget information
  • Remember language and display preferences
  • Provide and maintain normal App functionality
