Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Spin Wheel - Random Picker (the "App"), provided by JCraft Studio, handles information when users access its features and related services.

The App is a random selection and decision-making utility that allows users to enter options, spin a wheel, obtain random results, and use supported saving or history features.

This policy describes the types of information that may be processed, the purposes of processing, local storage practices, advertising services, and available privacy rights.

Important Privacy Notice

Wheel options, saved items, recent results, and application settings may be stored locally on the user's device.

Advertising services such as Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads may independently process device, network, advertising, and diagnostic information.

1. Information We Collect or Process

The App is designed to minimize unnecessary data collection. Account registration is not required for its basic random selection features.

The Developer does not require users to provide names, email addresses, phone numbers, government-issued identification numbers, or payment details to use the core wheel feature.

1.1 User-Entered Information

  • Wheel options or items entered by users.
  • Saved lists or wheel configurations, where supported.
  • Recently generated random results.
  • User preferences and language settings.

Such information may be processed locally to provide random selection, saved-item management, and result-history features.

1.2 Advertising-Related Information

Third-party advertising services may process the following categories of technical information:

  • Android Advertising ID or other permitted advertising identifiers.
  • IP address and approximate IP-derived location information.
  • Device manufacturer, model, operating system, and version.
  • Device language and relevant technical configuration.
  • Advertising impressions, clicks, views, and related interactions.
  • Diagnostic information, technical events, or SDK error reports.
  • Information relevant to advertising security and fraud prevention.

The information actually processed depends on SDK versions, connected advertising networks, device settings, applicable laws, and user privacy choices.

2. How Information Is Used

Information may be processed for the following purposes:

  • Providing wheel spinning and random selection features.
  • Managing saved wheel items and recent selection results.
  • Remembering preferences, including language settings.
  • Maintaining App functionality, performance, and stability.
  • Delivering advertisements and measuring ad performance.
  • Managing advertising frequency and related technical settings.
  • Preventing abuse, maintaining security, and detecting fraud.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations.

3. Local Storage on Your Device

The App may store wheel items, saved lists, recent results, and preferences locally on the user's device.

Local storage is used to support the App's functions and remember information between sessions.

3.1 Local Data

  • Entered wheel options and saved configurations.
  • Recent random selection records.
  • App and language preferences.
  • Local values used to manage advertising frequency.

These categories of App data are intended to remain on the user's device rather than being uploaded to a separate server operated by the Developer.

3.2 Data Deletion

Where the App provides deletion functions, users may remove saved items or result records through those functions.

Users may also clear App data through Android settings or uninstall the App.

These actions generally remove locally stored application data. However, some information may be restored through device backup and restoration features.

3.3 Data Retention

Locally saved information may remain on the device until it is deleted by the user, removed through App settings, or affected by device storage management.

Advertising-related information independently processed by third parties is subject to their respective retention and deletion policies.

4. Advertising Services

The App uses Unity LevelPlay for advertising mediation and Unity Ads for advertisement delivery.

4.1 Unity LevelPlay

Unity LevelPlay is an advertising mediation platform used to manage ad requests, advertising networks, ad formats, and monetization reporting.

Technical information may be processed to operate the mediation service and measure advertising performance.

4.2 Unity Ads

Unity Ads is an advertising network used to provide advertisements within the App.

Unity Ads and related advertising technology partners may process data for advertisement delivery, performance measurement, security, and fraud prevention.
