1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Glucose Note ("the App", "we", "our", or "the developer") handles information when users use the App.

The App is designed to help users manually record and manage information such as blood glucose readings, measurement times, weight, notes, reminders, statistics, and related personal health records.

Important Medical Disclaimer

Glucose Note is not a medical device, blood glucose meter, diagnostic system, medical service, or healthcare provider.

The App does not diagnose medical conditions, recommend treatment, prescribe medication, determine medication dosage, or replace professional medical advice.

Users are solely responsible for the accuracy of the information they enter into the App and for decisions made based on that information. Users should consult a qualified healthcare professional regarding medical conditions, diagnosis, treatment, medication, or emergencies.

2. Information Entered by Users

Users may voluntarily enter information including:

  • Blood glucose readings
  • Measurement dates and times
  • Measurement categories such as fasting or after meals
  • Body weight
  • User-created notes or memos
  • Reminder schedules
  • Target glucose ranges
  • Application preferences and settings

This information is entered voluntarily by the user and is used to provide the App's record, statistics, reminder, backup, PDF, and home-screen widget features.

3. Local Data Storage

User-entered glucose records, reminders, preferences, and related application data are primarily stored locally on the user's device.

The developer does not operate a separate account system, healthcare database, or cloud server for storing users' glucose records through this App.

Removing the App, clearing application data, resetting the device, losing the device, or device/storage failure may result in loss of local data.

Users are responsible for creating appropriate backups if they wish to preserve their records.

4. JSON Backup and Restore

The App allows users to create JSON backup files containing application records and settings.

The user selects where the backup file is stored using the Android system file interface. Backup files may contain information entered by the user, including health-related records.

Once a backup file is exported from the App, the user is responsible for managing, protecting, sharing, moving, or deleting that file.

The developer is not responsible for unauthorized access, disclosure, loss, corruption, or misuse of backup files stored, transferred, or shared outside the App.

5. PDF Reports and Sharing

The App can generate PDF reports containing user-entered glucose records and related information.

Users may choose to share a PDF using the Android system sharing interface or third-party applications installed on their device.

When a user chooses to share or export a PDF, the destination application or service may process the shared file according to its own privacy policy and terms.

The developer does not control how third-party applications or services handle files after the user chooses to share them.

6. Notifications and Reminders

The App may request notification permission in order to provide glucose measurement reminders.

Reminder schedules are created according to settings selected by the user.

Notification delivery may be affected by Android settings, battery optimization, device manufacturers, operating system restrictions, or user permission settings.

The developer does not guarantee that every reminder will be delivered at an exact time.

7. Home Screen Widget

The App may provide an Android home-screen widget that displays information such as:

  • The latest glucose reading
  • Measurement type
  • Measurement date or time
  • The next scheduled reminder

Information shown on the home-screen widget may be visible to anyone who can view or access the user's device.

Users should consider device security and lock-screen settings when using this feature.

8. Advertising and Google AdMob

The App may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

Google and its advertising technologies may collect or process information such as advertising identifiers, device or app information, IP-derived information, advertisement interaction data, diagnostics, and related technical information for purposes including advertising, fraud prevention, security, measurement, and service operation.

Such information is processed according to Google's own policies and applicable privacy choices.

Users should review Google's Privacy Policy, advertising policies, and device privacy settings for additional information regarding Google's data practices.

본 앱은 사용자가 직접 입력한 혈당 수치, 측정 시간, 체중, 메모, 알림, 통계 및 관련 건강 기록을 관리할 수 있도록 제공되는 도구입니다.

중요한 의료 관련 안내

본 앱은 의료기기, 혈당측정기, 의료 진단 시스템, 의료기관 또는 의료서비스가 아닙니다.

본 앱은 질병의 진단, 치료 권고, 의약품 처방, 투약량 결정 또는 의료 전문가의 진료를 대신하지 않습니다.

앱에 입력되는 정보의 정확성에 대한 책임은 사용자에게 있으며, 질병, 치료, 약물 복용, 건강 상태 또는 응급상황과 관련된 판단은 반드시 적절한 의료 전문가와 상담하시기 바랍니다.

2. 사용자가 직접 입력하는 정보

사용자는 다음과 같은 정보를 자발적으로 입력할 수 있습니다.

  • 혈당 측정값

사용자가 입력한 혈당 기록, 알림 설정, 앱 설정 및 관련 데이터는 기본적으로 사용자의 모바일 기기 내부에 저장됩니다.

앱 삭제, 앱 데이터 초기화, 기기 초기화, 기기 분실, 저장장치 손상 등의 상황에서는 저장된 데이터가 삭제될 수 있습니다.

본 앱은 앱 기록 및 설정을 JSON 파일로 백업할 수 있는 기능을 제공합니다.

사용자는 Android 시스템 파일 저장 기능을 통해 백업 파일을 저장할 위치를 직접 선택할 수 있습니다.

백업 파일이 앱 외부로 저장된 이후에는 해당 파일의 보관, 이동, 공유, 삭제 및 보안 관리 책임은 사용자에게 있습니다.
